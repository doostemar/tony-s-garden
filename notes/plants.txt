notes on plants

they obviously have states, so a state machine would be good here

SEED_STATE
SPROUT_STATE
PLANT_STATE

plants also have a GROWTH_VALUE, which helps determine change in state
growth starts at 0 and gradually goes up when the plant is planted, this is the GROWTH_RATE

RAIN affects GROWTH RATE, so plants need to keep track of WEATHER

the states themselves don't seem to have many properties, but mark the plant for animal interactions
	no animals eat seeds right now
	deer or rabbits will eat sprouts
	moles take finished plants

but what about pulled plants? perhaps this is the RADISH_STATE?
we give the plant collision for being picked up and thrown
