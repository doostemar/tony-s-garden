weather manager manages the weather

the weather states are SUNNY, CHANCE OF RAIN, and RAINING

the first two states only affect the UI. the player needs to know when it's
about to rain.

the states are changed by chance. the timer value for the next weather change is
randomized in a certain range. this keeps things fresh and relatively balanced.

the RAINING state is the only state that interacts with other game components

when it's raining, a signal will be emitted that increases the growth rate of
each dirt tile.

when the rain stops, the growth rate will revert back to its original value in
some way. perhaps it goes back gradually, which makes sense as the plants will
be "watered"d

the weather manager also needs to recieve a signal from tony's dance input. when
tony dances, the timer for the next rain needs to shorten dramatically.
