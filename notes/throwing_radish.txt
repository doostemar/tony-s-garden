let's think of a way to implement throwing a radish

implementation
	let's say tony has a reference to the radish in his hand
	so when he uses the throw command, he can send that radish data
	such as what direction tony is facing, and what his current throwing power is
	this will determine where the radish goes and how far it goes
	let's say it's velocity is always constant for now, the radish will move
	a certain amount of units per frame, in the direction tony was facing when
	he threw it, and it will go a distance determined by tony's throwing power

features
	i think though that radishes should do more when thrown than simply collide
	with the box or an enemy and then land, i think they should bounce, and they
	should always bounce to the nearest target
	for example, if tony throws a radish at an animal, if the nearest target is
	the harvest box, it will go to the harvest box, however if the nearest target
	is another animal, it will bounce to that animal and then bounce again, but
	it will only go as far as tony's throwing distance 
		(or maybe the bounce always grants a certain extra amount of distance, 
		which can be buffed, just like tony's power)
